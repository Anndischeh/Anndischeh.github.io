About me

I am a Machine Learning Engineer and AI researcher with an MSc in Artificial Intelligence and a background in Electrical and Electronic Engineering. My work combines deep learning, reinforcement learning, computer vision, NLP, and scientific and engineering applications of AI.

I have hands-on experience designing, implementing, and evaluating machine learning models using Python and PyTorch. My recent research focused on unsupervised anomaly detection in brain MRI, where I designed and benchmarked multiple deep learning architectures, including Autoencoders, VAEs, GANs, GANomaly, and a Vision Transformer-based model. I developed the complete experimental pipeline from data preprocessing and training to inference, evaluation, and visual analysis.

I also have practical experience in reinforcement learning, including Q-learning, DQN, PPO, and SAC. My work includes reward function design, systematic hyperparameter analysis, and reinforcement learning experiments across GridWorld, CartPole, and Atari Pong. I have used Ray RLlib for distributed reinforcement learning experiments with concurrent environment runners, while monitoring training performance and computational resources.

Beyond reinforcement learning and medical imaging, my projects include Transformer-based NLP with DistilBERT and computer vision with YOLO-based object detection. Across these projects, I focus on controlled experiments, quantitative evaluation, reproducibility, and building modular, configuration-driven research pipelines using Git and Weights & Biases.

My earlier research experience at EPFL and the University of Bern involved applied machine learning, multimodal and data-driven modelling, experimental evaluation, and collaboration with academic research groups. I also bring several years of university teaching and research experience, together with an engineering background in modelling, simulation, optimisation, and experimental validation.

I am particularly interested in machine learning and AI problems involving intelligent decision-making, representation learning, computer vision, multimodal and scientific AI, reinforcement learning, and reliable experimental ML systems.

You can find my projects on GitHub and experiment logs on Weights & Biases . I am open to research and engineering opportunities in AI and machine learning.
