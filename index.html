    • About me

    I am a Machine Learning Engineer and AI researcher with an MSc in Artificial Intelligence from City, University of London , and an MSc in Electrical & Electronic Engineering from the University of Tehran . My work combines deep learning, reinforcement learning, computer vision, NLP, and scientific and engineering applications of AI.

    My recent research focused on unsupervised anomaly detection in brain MRI. I designed and benchmarked multiple deep learning architectures, including Autoencoders, Variational Autoencoders, GANs, GANomaly, and a Vision Transformer-based model. I developed an end-to-end PyTorch pipeline covering data preprocessing, training, inference, evaluation, and anomaly visualisation, with systematic experimentation and quantitative model comparison.

    I also have hands-on experience in reinforcement learning, including Q-learning, DQN, PPO, and SAC. My work includes reward function design, hyperparameter analysis, and controlled experiments across GridWorld, CartPole, and Atari Pong. I have used Ray RLlib for distributed reinforcement learning experiments with concurrent environment runners, while monitoring training performance, environment throughput, and computational resources.

    Beyond reinforcement learning and medical imaging, my projects include Transformer-based NLP with DistilBERT and computer vision with YOLO-based object detection. Across these projects, I focus on experimental design, benchmarking, reproducibility, quantitative evaluation, and building modular and configuration-driven ML pipelines using Python, PyTorch, Git, and Weights & Biases.

    My earlier research experience includes visiting research positions at EPFL and the University of Bern , where I worked on applied machine learning, multimodal and data-driven modelling, experimental evaluation, and collaboration with academic research groups.

    My engineering background spans electrical and electronic systems, computational modelling, simulation, optimisation, and experimental validation. My earlier research included RF and antenna system design using electromagnetic simulation, followed by physical fabrication and experimental validation. This combination of engineering and machine learning gives me a systems-oriented approach to research and problem solving.

    I also have several years of university teaching and research experience at the University of Tehran , where I taught engineering and electronics-related subjects, supervised research projects, and communicated technical concepts to students and researchers with different levels of expertise.

    I am particularly interested in machine learning and AI problems involving intelligent decision-making, reinforcement learning, deep representation learning, computer vision, multimodal AI, scientific and engineering AI, and reliable experimental ML systems.

    You can find my projects on GitHub and experiment logs on Weights & Biases . I am open to research and engineering opportunities in artificial intelligence and machine learning.
