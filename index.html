About me

I am a Machine Learning Engineer with a strong background in building end-to-end ML systems, research-driven experimentation, and reproducible machine learning pipelines.

I am currently completing an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at City, University of London, where my work focuses on designing, training, and evaluating deep learning models across computer vision, NLP, and reinforcement learning.

My recent work includes developing end-to-end ML pipelines in PyTorch for medical image anomaly detection, benchmarking multiple deep learning architectures, and building reusable tooling for experimentation, evaluation, and reproducibility. I have hands-on experience with experiment tracking, configuration-driven experiments, and systematic analysis of training dynamics.

Previously, I worked as a Visiting Researcher at EPFL and the University of Bern, collaborating with AI and machine learning research groups on experimental and data-driven modeling. I also have several years of experience as a lecturer, supervising research projects and teaching engineering systems with a strong emphasis on analytical thinking and experimentation.

My interests lie at the intersection of machine learning research and engineering, particularly in ML systems, research tooling, and infrastructure that enable rapid, reliable experimentation and deployment.

You can find my projects on GitHub and experiment logs on Weights & Biases. I am always open to research collaborations and ML engineering opportunities.
